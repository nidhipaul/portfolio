How to read these numbers

Each ecosystem is measured in its own terms (reef area degraded, forest cleared, summer ice volume lost, mangrove area lost, grassland converted). All are percentages, so they share a 0–100% scale, but they are not the same quantity.

Tipping ranges are scientific estimates with wide uncertainty.
